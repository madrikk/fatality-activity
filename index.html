How to read this — click to expand

Each row is a guild member. Each column is 15 minutes of the day (00:00 on the left through 23:45 on the right, in ).

  • Darker gold — online at that time more often, and more recently. The number in the tooltip is the share of tracked days they were on at that time, weighted so recent days count for more.
  • Plain grey — a scan ran and they were offline.
  • Hatched — no scan covered that time, so nothing is known. This is deliberately different from grey: a missed scan should never look like someone being inactive.
  • Teal bar at the top — the times of day scans actually ran.

A one-off session fades out over about 5 days; a repeated one keeps darkening. So the map shows habits, not one-time appearances.

Controls: filter to weekdays or weekends, sort by time of day to see the guild's daily wave, search for a member, or switch to Roster table for power, rank, contribution and totals.

Activity is inferred from the member list's own “last seen” timer, so a member who logged off between scans is still placed correctly. Time bridged between two sightings is shown fainter than time actually observed.
